# llemonAI Bare Acts

Central and state Acts from India Code, as PDFs you can read and mark up, on Windows, Android and macOS.

**Not a government app.** llemonAI Bare Acts is made by llemonAI and is not connected with, or endorsed by, the Government of India, any state government or India Code. Every Act is downloaded from [India Code](https://indiacode.gov.in), the Government of India's public database of laws, and shown as India Code publishes it. India Code can take some time to include recent amendments: check the official Gazette before relying on a text. Nothing in the app is legal advice.

This repository holds the app's installers only.

## Download

Get the files from the [latest release](../../releases/latest):

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) | `llemonAI-Bare-Acts-1.0.0.exe` (runs as is, no installer) |
| Android 8 and later | `llemonAI-Bare-Acts-1.0.0-android.apk` |
| macOS 12 and later (Apple Silicon and Intel) | `llemonAI-Bare-Acts-1.0.0-macos.dmg` (or the `.zip`) |

`SHA256SUMS-1.0.0.txt` lists each file's SHA-256 checksum.

**macOS, first launch:** the app is not notarised, so macOS stops it the first time. Open **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to llemonAI Bare Acts.

## What it does

- Every central Act on India Code, the Constitution of India, and the repealed IPC, CrPC, Indian Evidence Act and Income-tax Act, 1961.
- State Acts for every state and union territory: pick the state once.
- Highlight, underline, pen and notes, kept with each Act automatically. Search inside an Act.
- An Act you open stays on your device and opens later without the internet. Once a week the app checks India Code for newer texts.

## Privacy

No account, no ads, no tracking. The app talks only to indiacode.gov.in, and your Acts and notes stay on your device.
